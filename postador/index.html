postador

postador is a personal, self-hosted tool that publishes short videos to the owner's own social media accounts (YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok) on a schedule.

It is operated by a single person for his own channels. It is not offered as a public service, and it does not access anyone else's accounts.

How it uses Google data

postador asks for permission to upload videos to the owner's YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. It uses that permission only to upload videos the owner chose and to set their title, description, tags and privacy.
