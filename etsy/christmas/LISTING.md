## Description

```
Christmas planner for Google Sheets and Excel: the gifts, the money, the cards, the dinner and every plan in December, with the days to Christmas.

Gifts:
- One line per gift with its price and what you paid
- Idea, bought, on the way, here and wrapped. An order that should have arrived turns red.
- A budget for each person, with what is spent, still to pay and left

Budget:
- Gifts, food and drink, decorations, cards, travel, outings and the rest
- What is spent, what is still to pay and what is left, category by category

Dinner:
- The menu and who makes each dish
- Type the time you sit down and how long each dish takes. The plan tells you when to prepare, cook and rest each one.
- The guests, with their diet or allergies

December:
- Parties, school events and travel on a list, and on a month calendar you can print

Cards:
- Who gets one, the address, and which ones are posted

Dashboard:
- Days to Christmas, gifts bought and wrapped, cards sent
- Who you still have to buy for, orders on the way and what is coming up
- The budget by category, with a chart

Room for 60 people, 300 gifts and 150 plans. Works in Google Sheets (free) and Microsoft Excel, in any currency. Next year, change the year and use it again.

You get:
- Christmas-Planner.xlsx
- Start-Here-Guide.pdf

This is a digital download and nothing will be shipped. The example family and their plans are made up.

I designed this with the help of AI tools and checked the formulas with the example data before listing it.
```
