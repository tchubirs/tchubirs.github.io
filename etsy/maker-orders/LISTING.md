## Description

```
Order tracker for Google Sheets and Excel, for people who make what they sell: candles, soap, jewelry, crochet, prints, cakes, wood and more.

Orders:
- One line per order, with the customer, product, quantity, extras such as a custom label or rush fee, and shipping
- The total, what was paid so far and the balance still to collect, for deposits and pay-on-pickup orders
- The due date and the status (new, making, ready, sent, picked up or cancelled), with how many days are left
- The cost of materials, the postage and the fees of the place it was sold, and the profit of each order

Products and materials:
- Your products with their price
- How much of each material one item uses, so the cost, profit and margin of each product fill in by themselves
- Your materials with pack size, price and stock, and what the open orders still need

Dashboard:
- Open orders, due in the next 7 days, late orders and money owed to you
- Open orders listed with the soonest due first
- Sales, profit and number of orders this month
- Sales and profit by month, with a chart, and by sales channel
- A short list of what needs attention: late orders, balances, materials that are short

Due dates:
- A calendar of the month with how many orders are due each day and which are still open

Customers:
- Orders, spending, last order and what each customer owes

Room for 1,000 orders, 100 products, 200 materials and 500 customers. Works in Google Sheets (free) and Microsoft Excel, in any currency.

You get:
- Handmade-Order-Tracker.xlsx
- Start-Here-Guide.pdf

This is a digital download and nothing will be shipped. The fees in the example are rough, so type in what your own platforms charge. It does not give tax advice.

I designed this with the help of AI tools and checked the formulas with the example data before listing it.
```
