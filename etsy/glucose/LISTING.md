## Description

```
Blood sugar log and medicine tracker for Google Sheets and Excel, in mg/dL or mmol/L. It keeps a record: the ranges are the ones your doctor or nurse gives you, and it gives no medical advice.

Readings:
- The date, time and moment of the day: fasting, after meals, bedtime or night
- Each reading marked below, in or above the range you type for that moment
- Notes on food, exercise or how you felt

Medicines:
- Each medicine with its dose and up to four times a day
- Log each dose, and today's list shows what is taken and what is still to come

Dashboard:
- The last reading and the averages for today, the week and the month
- How many readings were below, in or above your range in the last 7 days
- The last two weeks as a table and a chart

Report:
- One page for an appointment: a summary for each moment, your medicines, and every reading day by day for up to 31 days
- Print it or save it as a PDF

Room for 1,500 readings. Works in Google Sheets (free) and Microsoft Excel.

You get:
- Blood-Sugar-Log.xlsx
- Start-Here-Guide.pdf

This is a digital download and nothing will be shipped. The example person and every number in it are made up. This is not medical advice: talk to your doctor or nurse about your numbers and your medicines.

I designed this with the help of AI tools and checked the formulas with the example data before listing it.
```
