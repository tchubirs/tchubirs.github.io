## Description

```
Spreadsheet for short-term rental hosts, for Google Sheets and Excel. Add one line per stay and see nights, occupancy, nightly rate, revenue and profit for each month and each property.

Bookings tab:
- One line per stay, from any booking site or direct
- Nights, payout and status are calculated: upcoming, staying now, checked out, or payout not received
- Payout means what the guest paid plus the cleaning fee, minus the platform fee

Expenses tab:
- One line per cost, for one property or shared between them
- Ten categories you can rename, such as cleaning, supplies, utilities and repairs

Dashboard:
- Pick the year and one property, or all of them
- Revenue, expenses, profit, nights booked, occupancy to date and average nightly rate
- A table and a chart by month. A stay that crosses a month end is split by nights, so each month shows its own numbers.
- Each property side by side, with shared costs shown apart
- Check-ins in the next 7 days, payouts not received yet, and the next check-outs with a note when a new guest arrives the same day

Room for 6 properties, 500 bookings and 500 expenses. Works in Google Sheets (free) and Microsoft Excel, in any currency.

You get:
- Vacation-Rental-Host-Tracker.xlsx
- Start-Here-Guide.pdf

This is a digital download and nothing will be shipped. The file tracks numbers and does not give tax advice.

I designed this with the help of AI tools and checked the formulas with the example data before listing it.
```
