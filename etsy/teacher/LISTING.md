## Description

```
Teacher planner and gradebook for Google Sheets and Excel: your timetable, your lessons week by week, a gradebook for each class and the students to watch.

Lessons:
- Your usual week once, with the class in each period
- Each lesson by date and period, with the topic, the aims and the homework; the class fills in from the timetable
- A week plan to print, with the lessons not planned yet shaded and the homework set that week

Gradebook:
- One tab for each class, up to six, with 40 students and 20 assessments each
- Each assessment with its date, marks out of and weight
- Weighted averages and grades from your own scale
- Missing work, excused work and marks not in yet kept apart
- Absences and late arrivals for each student

Dashboard:
- Today's lessons and topics
- How many lessons are planned this week and what is left to mark
- Each class with its average and how many are below the pass mark
- The students with the lowest averages, with their absences

Works in Google Sheets (free) and Microsoft Excel.

You get:
- Teacher-Planner-Gradebook.xlsx
- Start-Here-Guide.pdf

This is a digital download and nothing will be shipped. The example classes and students are made up.

I designed this with the help of AI tools and checked the formulas with the example data before listing it.
```
