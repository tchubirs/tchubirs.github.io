## Description

```
Loan tracker for Google Sheets and Excel, for money lent to or borrowed from friends and family: who owes what, the next payments, what is overdue, and a statement for each loan.

Loans:
- Each loan once, lent or borrowed, with its date, amount and notes
- An optional plan: so many payments every week, two weeks or month, from a first date
- Simple interest if you want it, so much a year
- What is still to pay, the next payment and its date, and the status: on track, due soon, overdue, open or paid off

Payments:
- Each payment as it happens, picked by the loan's number and name
- Money in and money out kept apart

Statement:
- Pick a loan to print or share it: the amount, what was paid, what is left and the next payment
- A friendly reminder message ready to copy and send

Dashboard:
- What you are owed and what you owe, and what is overdue each way
- The next payments in date order
- Each person's balance
- Money in and out by month, with a chart

Room for 300 loans and 800 payments. Works in Google Sheets (free) and Microsoft Excel, in any currency.

You get:
- Personal-Loan-Tracker.xlsx
- Start-Here-Guide.pdf

This is a digital download and nothing will be shipped. The example people and loans are made up.

I designed this with the help of AI tools and checked the formulas with the example data before listing it.
```
