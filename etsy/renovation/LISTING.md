## Description

```
Home renovation budget spreadsheet for Google Sheets and Excel. Set a budget for each room, compare contractor quotes, see which payments are due and plan the work week by week.

Budget tab:
- A budget for each room or area, plus a contingency you choose (10% in the example)
- Committed, paid, still to pay and what is left for each room
- A status that turns orange near the limit and red when a room goes over

Costs tab:
- One line per quote, job or purchase
- Quotes for the same job are compared: the sheet marks the cheapest and shows how much more the others cost
- A line only counts against the budget once you mark it Hired or Bought
- A payment status for each hired job: paid, due soon, overdue, or the date of the next payment

Timeline tab:
- One line per task with a start date, an end date and a status (planned, in progress, done or delayed)
- The bars fill in by themselves, one column per week, with the current week highlighted

Dashboard:
- Total budget, committed, paid, still to pay and what is left to spend
- The next five payments, with overdue ones in red
- How many jobs are still waiting for a decision between quotes, and the next task

Works in Google Sheets (free) and Microsoft Excel, in any currency. Room for 12 rooms, 300 quotes and costs, and 30 tasks.

You get:
- Home-Renovation-Budget-Planner.xlsx
- Start-Here-Guide.pdf

This is a digital download and nothing will be shipped.

I designed this with the help of AI tools and checked the formulas with the example data before listing it.
```
