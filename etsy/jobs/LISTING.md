## Description

```
Job application tracker for Google Sheets and Excel: every job from saved to the answer, the next step for each one, your interviews, and each week against your goal.

Applications:
- The company, role, place, pay, source and the day you applied
- The stage it has reached and the day of the last contact
- A next step with its date, or a follow-up date that comes by itself
- A job that has been quiet too long is marked No reply

Interviews:
- Each call, video, visit or task with what to prepare and how it went
- A reminder to send the thank-you note

Contacts:
- Recruiters, referrals and people who can help, with the next time to get in touch

Dashboard:
- How many you applied for, this week against your goal, how many got a reply, interviews and offers
- The pipeline stage by stage
- The next steps in date order, and the interviews coming
- The last eight weeks with a chart

Room for 300 jobs. Works in Google Sheets (free) and Microsoft Excel, in any currency.

You get:
- Job-Application-Tracker.xlsx
- Start-Here-Guide.pdf

This is a digital download and nothing will be shipped. The example search, its companies and people are made up.

I designed this with the help of AI tools and checked the formulas with the example data before listing it.
```
