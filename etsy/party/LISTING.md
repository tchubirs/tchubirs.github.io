## Description

```
Party planner for Google Sheets and Excel, for birthdays, baby showers, graduations, anniversaries, holiday parties and any event you host.

Dashboard:
- A countdown to the day
- Who is coming, who may come and who has not answered, in adults and kids
- The next tasks, with the late ones first
- The money: planned, paid and still to pay, and how much of your budget is left
- The dietary needs of the guests who are coming

Tasks:
- A checklist of 27 tasks, from 8 weeks before to the days after
- Each task is dated back from the day of the party. Change the date and they all move.
- Each task marked Done, This week or Late

Guests:
- One line per guest or family: answer, adults, kids, dietary needs and notes
- Gifts, and the thank-you notes still to send

Food and drinks:
- Type how much each adult and each kid eats or drinks, and the size and price of a pack
- It works out how much to buy for everyone who may come, with a little extra, in whole packs

Budget:
- The venue, cake, entertainment and the rest, with deposits, what is still to pay and by when

The day:
- The plan for the party hour by hour, and who looks after what

Room for 300 guests, 60 tasks and 50 costs. Works in Google Sheets (free) and Microsoft Excel, in any currency.

You get:
- Party-Planner.xlsx
- Start-Here-Guide.pdf

This is a digital download and nothing will be shipped. The example party, its guests and its shops are made up.

I designed this with the help of AI tools and checked the formulas with the example data before listing it.
```
