## Description

```
Software and subscription tracker for a small business, in Google Sheets and Excel: every tool the business pays for, who uses it, what renews next, and what to do when someone leaves.

Tools:
- Monthly, quarterly or yearly plans, per seat or at a flat price
- The next charge moves on by itself after each renewal
- A notice period gives the last day to cancel before a yearly renewal
- Seats bought against seats used, and what the unused ones cost a year

Access and team:
- One line per person and tool, with who is admin
- Type the day someone leaves: every tool they can still get into is listed

Cards:
- Each card or account with the tools on it and what they cost
- A card that expires before a renewal is flagged, so a charge does not fail

Year ahead:
- Every charge for the next twelve months, tool by tool and month by month

Dashboard:
- What the tools cost a month, a year and per person
- What is due in the next 30 days, what needs a look, and the cost of each category
- A chart of the charges over the next twelve months

Room for 100 tools, 100 people and 400 access lines. Works in Google Sheets (free) and Microsoft Excel, in any currency.

You get:
- Software-Tools-Tracker.xlsx
- Start-Here-Guide.pdf

This is a digital download and nothing will be shipped. The example business, its team and tools are made up. Keep passwords out of the file.

I designed this with the help of AI tools and checked the formulas with the example data before listing it.
```
