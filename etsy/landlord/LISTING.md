## Description

```
Landlord spreadsheet for Google Sheets and Excel, for people who rent out one or more homes or flats by the month.

Units tab:
- One line per unit, with the tenant, rent, due day, grace days, lease dates and deposit
- A lease status that updates by itself: active, ends within 60 days, lease ended or empty

Rent log:
- One line each time rent arrives, with the unit, amount and method
- A payment made early or late can count for another month

Rent roll:
- Every unit and every month of the year in one grid
- Green when paid in full, orange when part paid, red when late
- Rent owed now for each unit, and this month's status: paid, part paid, late or due on a given day

Expenses tab:
- One line per cost, with its property and category (mortgage interest, insurance, property tax, repairs and others you can rename)

Dashboard:
- Rent received, rent owed now, expenses, net income, units let and deposits held
- A table and a chart by month
- Each property on its own line
- Late rent, leases that end in the next 60 days and empty units

Room for 6 properties, 20 units, 1,000 payments and 1,000 expenses. Works in Google Sheets (free) and Microsoft Excel, in any currency.

You get:
- Landlord-Rental-Tracker.xlsx
- Start-Here-Guide.pdf

This is a digital download and nothing will be shipped. The file tracks numbers and does not give tax or legal advice.

I designed this with the help of AI tools and checked the formulas with the example data before listing it.
```
