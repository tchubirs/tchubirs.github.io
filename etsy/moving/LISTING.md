## Description

```
Moving planner for Google Sheets and Excel, for moving house or flat, across town or to a new city.

Dashboard:
- A countdown to moving day
- The next tasks, with the late ones first
- Boxes by room: how many, packed, fragile and arrived
- The boxes to open first, and the next free box number
- The money: planned, paid and still to pay, and how much of your budget is left
- How many people and services already have your new address

Tasks:
- A checklist of 28 tasks, from 8 weeks before moving day to 3 weeks after
- Each task is dated back from moving day. Change the date and they all move.

Boxes:
- One line per box: the number you write on it, the room it leaves, the room it goes to and what is inside
- Mark it fragile or open first, then packed and arrived

Movers:
- Quotes side by side, with the cheapest in green and dates that are not moving day in orange

Costs:
- Every cost with the deposit, what is still to pay and by when

New address:
- The bank, work, doctor, insurance, utilities, post and the rest, ticked off as you tell them
- The meter readings to take on moving day

Room for 300 boxes, 70 tasks and 50 costs. Works in Google Sheets (free) and Microsoft Excel, in any currency. In Google Sheets you can share it with whoever moves with you.

You get:
- Moving-Planner.xlsx
- Start-Here-Guide.pdf

This is a digital download and nothing will be shipped. The example move, its companies and its places are made up.

I designed this with the help of AI tools and checked the formulas with the example data before listing it.
```
