## Description

```
Social media content planner for Google Sheets and Excel: every post from the idea to the results, a month calendar to print, and each platform against its weekly goal.

Posts:
- Each post with its date, time, platform, type and topic
- Its status as it moves along: idea, draft, scheduled, published
- Ideas can wait without a date
- Once it is out, its reach, likes, comments, shares and saves, with the rate worked out
- Posts that missed their date show as late

Calendar:
- A month of posts to print, three to a day with the platform's short name
- Any month you choose

Dashboard:
- The posts coming up, what is late and the ideas waiting
- Each platform this week against its goal, and its rate this month
- The best posts of the month
- The posts published each week, with a chart
- Your followers from the Stats tab

Stats:
- Followers on each platform at the end of each month, with the growth

Room for 800 posts and 8 platforms. Works in Google Sheets (free) and Microsoft Excel.

You get:
- Content-Planner.xlsx
- Start-Here-Guide.pdf

This is a digital download and nothing will be shipped. The example shop, its posts and its numbers are made up.

I designed this with the help of AI tools and checked the formulas with the example data before listing it.
```
