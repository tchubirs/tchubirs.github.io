## Description

```
Spreadsheet for Etsy sellers that works out every fee on a sale and what you keep. For Google Sheets and Excel.

Calculator tab:
- Type the item price, the shipping you charge, a discount and your costs
- See each fee: listing, transaction, payment processing, Offsite Ads, regulatory fee, currency conversion and VAT on fees
- Your profit and margin, and the item price that reaches the margin you aim for

Products tab:
- One line per product, up to 200
- Fees, profit and margin for each, with a status: on target, below target or loss
- A suggested price for your target margin

Settings tab:
- Rates for the United States, United Kingdom, France, Germany, Italy, Spain, Canada and Australia
- Every rate can be changed, so the file stays right when Etsy changes its fees
- Your hourly rate, so the time each item takes counts as a cost

Works in Google Sheets (free) and Microsoft Excel.

You get:
- Seller-Fee-Profit-Calculator.xlsx
- Start-Here-Guide.pdf

This is a digital download and nothing will be shipped. This file is not made or endorsed by Etsy. Check the current fees on Etsy's fee page before you set your prices.

I designed this with the help of AI tools and checked the formulas with the example data before listing it.
```
