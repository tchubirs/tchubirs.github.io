## Description

```
Net worth and dividend tracker for Google Sheets and Excel: your net worth month by month, your passive income against a goal, and what each holding pays.

Balances:
- Each account once: cash, savings, investments, retirement, property, vehicles, and debts such as cards, loans and a mortgage
- The balance of each account at the end of every month, for three years
- Assets, debts and net worth worked out for each month

Income:
- Each dividend, interest payment or rent as it comes in
- Each holding with what it paid over the last 12 months and this year, and its yield

Dashboard:
- Net worth and how it moved on the month before
- Assets and debts
- Passive income over the last 12 months, a month on average, and how far you are from your goal
- Net worth by month and passive income by month, with charts
- Your assets by type, and the holdings that pay you the most

Works in Google Sheets (free) and Microsoft Excel, in any currency.

You get:
- Net-Worth-Dividend-Tracker.xlsx
- Start-Here-Guide.pdf

This is a digital download and nothing will be shipped. The example accounts and amounts are made up. It keeps a record for your own use and gives no financial advice.

I designed this with the help of AI tools and checked the formulas with the example data before listing it.
```
