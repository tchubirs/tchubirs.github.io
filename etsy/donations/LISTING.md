## Description

```
Donation and volunteer tracker for Google Sheets and Excel, made for a small charity, church, food pantry, club or school group: every gift and who gave it, your campaigns against their goals, a statement of each donor's gifts to print, and the hours your volunteers give.

Gifts:
- Date, donor, amount, how it was given and the campaign
- Goods as well as money: leave the amount empty and say what was given
- An x once the donor has been thanked, and a To thank list until then
- A line that needs a look is flagged, like a gift with no date or a donor who is not on the list yet

Donors:
- Each donor once, with their type, email and address
- First and last gift, what they gave this year and in all
- Who is new this year, and who has lapsed after the number of months you choose

Campaigns:
- A goal and dates if it has them, what it has raised and how many donors gave
- Days left, and whether it is running, ended or has reached its goal

Statement:
- Pick a donor and a year: their gifts with the total and your own closing line, on one page to print or save as a PDF

Volunteers:
- Each time someone helps, with the hours
- Hours this year and in all, the last time they came and how often

Dashboard:
- Given this year, the number of gifts, the average gift, donors, new and lapsed donors
- The campaigns against their goals and the thank-you notes still to send
- Each month's giving and hours, the top donors and a chart

Room for 1,000 gifts, 300 donors, 20 campaigns and 100 volunteers. Works in Google Sheets (free) and Microsoft Excel, in any currency.

The statement lists the gifts you received. Whether a gift can be claimed on taxes, and what a receipt has to say, depends on the rules where you are.

You get:
- Donation-Volunteer-Tracker.xlsx
- Start-Here-Guide.pdf

This is a digital download and nothing will be shipped. The example pantry, its donors and volunteers are made up.

I designed this with the help of AI tools and checked the formulas with the example data before listing it.
```
