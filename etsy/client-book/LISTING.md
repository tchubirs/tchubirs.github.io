## Description

```
Client and appointment tracker for Google Sheets and Excel, for people who work one to one: hair stylists, barbers, nail and beauty techs, personal trainers, tutors, therapists and coaches.

Appointments:
- One line per booking, with the date, time, client and service
- The price comes from your list of services, minus any discount
- Booked, done, no-show or cancelled, with what was paid and how
- What is still to pay, and an orange flag on a past booking you forgot to update

Clients:
- One line per client, with phone, email, birthday and notes
- Visits, last visit, next booking and what each client has paid in total
- What they owe you, package sessions left, and who has not been back for a while

Packages:
- Prepaid session cards, for example 5 blow dries or 10 training sessions
- Each visit paid with the package uses one session

Week view:
- The seven days of any week, each appointment in time order
- Green when done, blue when booked, red for a no-show, and what each day brought in

Dashboard:
- Paid this month, visits done, bookings ahead, no-shows and money owed to you
- The next 10 appointments
- A list of what needs attention: unpaid visits, packages running out, clients to follow up, birthdays this month
- A table and a chart of what you were paid each month

Room for 300 clients, 2,000 appointments, 300 packages and 30 services. Works in Google Sheets (free) and Microsoft Excel, in any currency.

You get:
- Client-Appointment-Tracker.xlsx
- Start-Here-Guide.pdf

This is a digital download and nothing will be shipped. It is a spreadsheet, so it does not send reminders or take online bookings.

I designed this with the help of AI tools and checked the formulas with the example data before listing it.
```
