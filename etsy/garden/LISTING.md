## Description

```
Vegetable garden planner for Google Sheets and Excel: what you sow and plant in each bed, when it should be ready, each pick valued at shop prices, the watering, what the garden cost, and a planting calendar to print.

Crops:
- Each crop with its family, unit, the shop price you would pay, and the days to harvest from the seed packet
- The months you plan to sow, plant out and harvest it, also across the new year
- The seeds you have left, and what each crop gave this year

Plantings:
- Each sowing or planting with its date and bed
- When it should be ready, from your own days to harvest
- Growing, picking or done, and how long it has been in the ground
- A note when a bed gets a crop of the same family as last year

Harvest:
- Each pick with its amount, valued at the crop's shop price

Beds and jobs:
- What grows in each bed and the latest planting
- Watering and other jobs, the last watering of each bed, and the beds that need water now

Costs:
- Seeds, plants, compost, tools and water, month by month

Calendar:
- Your crops and the twelve months on one page: S sow, P plant out, H harvest

Dashboard:
- The harvest value, what the garden cost, and the value less the costs
- The crops that gave the most this year
- What to sow, plant out and harvest this month
- The beds with their last watering, and a chart of each month

Room for 60 crops, 30 beds, 500 plantings and 1,500 picks. Works in Google Sheets (free) and Microsoft Excel, in any currency and any unit.

The months and days are the ones you type: they depend on where you live and on the weather, so the sheet does not work out planting dates for you.

You get:
- Vegetable-Garden-Planner.xlsx
- Start-Here-Guide.pdf

This is a digital download and nothing will be shipped. The example garden and its months are made up.

I designed this with the help of AI tools and checked the formulas with the example data before listing it.
```
