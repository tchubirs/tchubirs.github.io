## Description

```
Batch and expiry tracker for Google Sheets and Excel, for home bakers, cottage food sellers and small food makers: batch codes, best-before dates, labels with the allergens, the cost of each item and the waste.

Batches:
- One line for each batch, with a batch code and a best-before date worked out from the shelf life
- What was sold, given away and thrown out, and what is left
- Use soon and past its date in colour, and a list of what to sell first

Labels:
- Pick a batch and print six labels to a page
- The ingredients in order of amount, the allergens, the batch code, the dates, the net weight and your business details
- Your own wording, such as Best before or Use by, and the line your local rules ask for

Recipes and costs:
- Pack prices and recipes give the cost of a batch and of each item, the profit and the margin
- Packaging counts in the cost and stays off the label

Ingredients:
- An x under each allergen, once, and every product and label picks it up
- Purchases with the lot number and the date on the pack
- What is left of each ingredient and what to buy

Dashboard:
- Items in stock, to use soon and past their date
- What to sell first, what to buy and what is going off in the pantry
- Made and wasted by month, with a chart

Room for 40 products, 100 ingredients and 500 batches. Works in Google Sheets (free) and Microsoft Excel, in any currency.

You get:
- Food-Batch-Tracker.xlsx
- Start-Here-Guide.pdf

This is a digital download and nothing will be shipped. The example bakery, its recipes and prices are made up. The labels put together what you type: check them against the food rules where you sell.

I designed this with the help of AI tools and checked the formulas with the example data before listing it.
```
