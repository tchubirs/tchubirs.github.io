## Description

```
Personal trainer client tracker for Google Sheets and Excel: session packages, no-shows and late cancellations, what each client owes, their measurements and marks at each check-in, and a progress report to print for each client.

Clients:
- Each client with the day they started, their goal and their status
- Sessions attended, this month and missed
- Sessions bought, used and left, the last session and the next check-in
- What each client owes, and what needs your attention

Packages:
- Each block of sessions with the price, what was paid and the date to use it by
- The price per session and what is still owed

Sessions:
- Book ahead, then mark each one attended, no-show, late cancel or cancelled
- A no-show uses a session, and a late cancel too if you choose

Check-ins:
- Weight, body fat, waist, hips, chest, arm and thigh, in your own units
- Three performance marks you name yourself, such as a lift, a count or a time

Progress report:
- Pick a client: each measure and mark from the first check-in to the latest, the best mark, attendance, and their weight over time
- One page to print or save as a PDF

Dashboard:
- Active clients, sessions and missed sessions this month, what you sold and what you are owed
- The clients running out of sessions, owing money, with a package ending or due a check-in
- The next sessions booked, and each month with a chart

Room for 100 clients, 3,000 sessions and 1,500 check-ins. Works in Google Sheets (free) and Microsoft Excel, in any currency and any units.

You get:
- Personal-Trainer-Client-Tracker.xlsx
- Start-Here-Guide.pdf

This is a digital download and nothing will be shipped. The example studio and its clients are made up. The file records numbers and gives no health or training advice.

I designed this with the help of AI tools and checked the formulas with the example data before listing it.
```
