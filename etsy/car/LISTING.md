## Description

```
Car maintenance tracker for Google Sheets and Excel, for one car or a whole family of cars, vans and motorbikes.

Dashboard:
- Each car's odometer, consumption, what it cost this year and its cost per distance
- The services overdue or due soon, the overdue ones first
- Insurance, tax and inspection that end soon
- The year month by month, fuel and other costs, with a chart

Services:
- Every service by distance or by months, whichever comes first
- The next odometer and date, and the day the car should get there at its usual pace

Fuel:
- One line per fill-up. The consumption is worked out from one full tank to the next, so a half fill is fine.
- Litres per 100 km and km per litre, or miles per gallon

Costs:
- Services, repairs, tyres, insurance, tax, parking and tolls

Documents:
- Every paper with the day it ends, and a warning before

Up to 5 cars, 600 fill-ups and 500 costs. Works in Google Sheets (free) and Microsoft Excel, in km or miles, litres or gallons, and any currency.

You get:
- Car-Maintenance-Tracker.xlsx
- Start-Here-Guide.pdf

This is a digital download and nothing will be shipped. The example cars and their numbers are made up. Service intervals differ from car to car: the handbook or your garage has yours.

I designed this with the help of AI tools and checked the formulas with the example data before listing it.
```
