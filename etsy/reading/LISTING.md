## Description

```
Reading tracker for Google Sheets and Excel, for book lovers, reading challenges and book clubs.

Dashboard:
- Your goal for the year, with a bar that shows if you are ahead or behind for today's date
- Books and pages this year, your average rating, what you are reading now and how many you want to read
- Books and pages by month, with a chart
- Books, pages and average rating by genre
- Your five-star books of the year

Books:
- One line per book: title, author, genre, format (paper, ebook or audio), pages, status, dates, rating and a note
- The days each book took and your pages a day are worked out for you
- Want to read, Reading, Finished and Did not finish in the same list

Bookshelf:
- Every book you finish goes on a shelf, in the order you finished them and in the colour of its genre
- Room for 100 books a year

Settings:
- Twelve genres, each with its colour on the shelf. Rename them to the genres you read.

Room for 1,000 books. Change the year on the dashboard to look back at another year. Works in Google Sheets (free) and Microsoft Excel.

You get:
- Reading-Tracker.xlsx
- Start-Here-Guide.pdf

This is a digital download and nothing will be shipped. The example books and authors are made up.

I designed this with the help of AI tools and checked the formulas with the example data before listing it.
```
