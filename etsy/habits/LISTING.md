## Description

```
Habit and mood tracker for Google Sheets and Excel, for a whole year.

Month tabs:
- One tab for each month, with up to 15 habits and every day of the month
- Type x on the days you did a habit, or use checkboxes in Google Sheets
- A mood from 1 to 5 and the hours you slept, for every day
- For each habit: days done, share of days so far, the streak you are on and your best streak

Streaks:
- A streak counts the days in a row and carries on from one month to the next
- Your best streak of the year for every habit

Year in pixels:
- Every day of the year in the colour of your mood, one square per day

Dashboard:
- This month: the share of habits done, your longest streak going, your average mood and sleep
- Each habit with a bar, its streak now and its best streak this year
- The year month by month: mood, sleep and habits, with a chart
- A table of every habit and month, shaded from light to dark

Works in Google Sheets (free) and Microsoft Excel. For a new year, make a copy and change the year.

You get:
- Habit-Mood-Tracker.xlsx
- Start-Here-Guide.pdf

This is a digital download and nothing will be shipped. It is a personal tracker and does not give medical advice.

I designed this with the help of AI tools and checked the formulas with the example data before listing it.
```
