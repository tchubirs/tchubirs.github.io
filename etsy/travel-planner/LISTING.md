## Description

```
Travel planner for Google Sheets and Excel, for holidays, honeymoons, road trips and trips abroad.

Dashboard:
- A countdown to the first day, and the number of nights
- Your budget for each category, with what is booked, spent, paid and left
- The next bookings with their confirmation codes and what is still to pay
- The next plans of your itinerary, day by day
- How much of your packing is done

Bookings:
- Every flight, hotel, train, tour and restaurant, with dates, time and confirmation code
- The cost and what you have paid, in any currency, turned into your home currency
- Each booking counts in the budget as soon as you add it

Itinerary:
- What you plan to do each day, with the time, the place and the booking it needs
- Type the lines in any order: the dashboard puts them in order

Expenses:
- Everything you pay that is not a booking, in any currency

Packing list:
- Forty things most trips need, by kind, with how many of each
- Type x as things go in the bag, and add your own

Room for 100 bookings, 300 plans, 500 expenses and 250 packing items. Works in Google Sheets (free) and Microsoft Excel.

You get:
- Travel-Planner.xlsx
- Start-Here-Guide.pdf

This is a digital download and nothing will be shipped. The exchange rates are the ones you type.

I designed this with the help of AI tools and checked the formulas with the example data before listing it.
```
