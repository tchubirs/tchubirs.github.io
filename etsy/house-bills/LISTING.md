## Description

```
Bill splitter for Google Sheets and Excel, for roommates, shared houses, student houses and couples who share the bills.

Dashboard (pick a month):
- What each housemate paid and their share of the bills
- Each balance, and the fewest payments that settle everyone up, for example Priya pays Maya 1,197.07
- The bills due that month, marked Paid, Due soon or Late
- What the house spent by category, and the next bill due

Bills:
- Each bill once: how often it comes (every month, every 2, 3 or 6 months, once a year or when needed) and the day it is due
- Split it equally, by shares like the size of each room, or only between some of you
- The next due date of every bill, in red when it is late

Payments:
- One line each time someone pays a bill or buys something for the house
- It uses the bill's split, or you mark who shares a one-off
- Paying each other back counts too

Housemates:
- Up to 8 people. When someone moves in or out part of the way through, type the date and the bills after it are split without them.

Chores:
- A rota that moves every Monday, so everyone takes a turn

Year:
- Every month by category, with a chart, and what each housemate paid and shared

Room for 20 bills and 600 payments. Works in Google Sheets (free) and Microsoft Excel, in any currency. In Google Sheets you can share it so everyone adds what they pay from their phone.

You get:
- Roommate-Bill-Splitter.xlsx
- Start-Here-Guide.pdf

This is a digital download and nothing will be shipped. It does not send or take payments; it tells you who pays whom. The example house and its people are made up.

I designed this with the help of AI tools and checked the formulas with the example data before listing it.
```
