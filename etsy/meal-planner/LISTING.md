## Description

```
Weekly meal planner for Google Sheets and Excel. You pick a recipe for each meal and the grocery list builds itself.

Week plan:
- Seven days and four meals a day (breakfast, lunch, dinner and snack, or names of your own)
- The number of people eating each day, so the amounts follow
- A line for prep notes, like moving something from the freezer to the fridge
- The shopping cost of the week, the cost per person per day, and checks that tell you when something is missing

Grocery list:
- Only what the week needs, minus what you already have at home
- Rounded up to whole packs, for example 3 dozen eggs or 2 boxes of spaghetti
- In the order of your store, section by section, with the cost of each item and the total
- A column to tick things off as you shop, on paper or on your phone

Recipes and ingredients:
- One line per recipe, with how many people it serves, the minutes and a link or note
- One line per ingredient, in the unit you cook with (cup, oz, g, tbsp, each)
- The cost per serving of each recipe, from your own prices

Pantry:
- Every food you buy, with its store section, pack and price
- What you have at home, and extra packs for things you buy anyway, like coffee or dish soap

Room for 100 recipes, 1,000 ingredient lines and 300 pantry items. Works in Google Sheets (free) and Microsoft Excel, with any currency and any unit. It comes with 15 example recipes that you can keep or delete.

You get:
- Weekly-Meal-Planner.xlsx
- Start-Here-Guide.pdf

This is a digital download and nothing will be shipped.

I designed this with the help of AI tools and checked the formulas with the example data before listing it.
```
