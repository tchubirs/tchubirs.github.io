## Description

```
Expense splitter for Google Sheets and Excel, for trips with friends or family, shared houses, bachelor and bachelorette weekends, and any group that shares costs.

Expenses:
- One line per expense, with the date, what it was, a category, the amount, the currency and who paid
- Split it equally between everyone, only between some people, or in unequal shares
- Pay in any currency: each one is turned into your home currency with the rate you type
- Paying someone back during the trip counts too

Dashboard:
- What each person paid, their share of the costs and their balance: who gets money back and who owes
- Settle up: the fewest payments that bring every balance to zero, for example Emma pays Chloe 352.46
- The total, the cost per person and per day
- Spending by category, with a chart, and by day

Up to 12 people, 500 expenses and 10 currencies. Works in Google Sheets (free) and Microsoft Excel. In Google Sheets you can share it so everyone adds what they pay from their phone.

You get:
- Trip-Expense-Splitter.xlsx
- Start-Here-Guide.pdf

This is a digital download and nothing will be shipped. It does not send or take payments; it tells you who pays whom.

I designed this with the help of AI tools and checked the formulas with the example data before listing it.
```
