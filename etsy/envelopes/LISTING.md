## Description

```
Cash envelope budget for Google Sheets and Excel, for the cash stuffing way of budgeting: what goes into each envelope every payday, the notes to ask for at the bank, what you spend from each envelope, and what is left to carry over to the next month.

Envelopes:
- Up to 16 envelopes for spending and for sinking funds
- A plan for each payday, and the largest note you want in each envelope
- What went in, what was spent or moved, and what is in it now
- Low when less than a quarter of its plan is left, a share you can change
- A sinking fund can have a target and a date, and shows what it needs each payday to get there

Paydays:
- One line per payday, with what goes into each envelope
- A plan row with your usual amounts, and what stays in the bank for the bills

Cash to take out:
- The notes to ask for at the bank, worked out from what goes into each envelope
- How many of each note go into each envelope, and what is left in coins
- Works with the notes of any currency: you type the ones your bank gives out

Spending:
- One line each time you spend cash from an envelope
- Move leftovers to another envelope, such as your savings

Envelope cards:
- Eight cards to a page to print and keep in your envelopes, with the cash in each one on payday and lines to note what you spend

Dashboard:
- The month you pick: what each envelope had at the start, what went in, what came out and what is left
- Cash in your envelopes, left to spend and saved in sinking funds
- Your sinking funds against their targets, the latest spending, and a chart of each month

Works in Google Sheets (free) and Microsoft Excel, in any currency. Paid every week, every 2 weeks, twice a month or every month.

You get:
- Cash-Envelope-Budget.xlsx
- Start-Here-Guide.pdf

This is a digital download and nothing will be shipped. The example envelopes and amounts are made up. It is a budgeting tool and does not give financial advice.

I designed this with the help of AI tools and checked the formulas with the example data before listing it.
```
