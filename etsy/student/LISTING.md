## Description

```
Student planner for Google Sheets and Excel, for high school, college and university.

Assignments and exams:
- Every assignment, quiz and exam with its course, kind and due date
- To do, in progress or done, the score and what it was out of
- How many days are left, and a red flag on anything late

Courses and grades:
- Each course with its credits and how much homework, quizzes, projects, the midterm, the final and anything else count
- Your grade so far in every course, weighted the way your teacher grades, with the letter
- The score you need on the final to reach the grade you are aiming for
- A GPA from your own grade scale, which you can change

Study log:
- One line each time you study, with the minutes
- Hours per week against your goal, and hours per course

Timetable:
- The hours of the week you have class, each course in its own colour

Dashboard:
- Your GPA, what is due in the next 7 days, what is late and your study hours this week
- Every course with its grade, letter, target and the score needed on the final
- The next 10 things due
- A chart of your study hours by week

Room for 10 courses, 500 assignments and 1,000 study sessions. Works in Google Sheets (free) and Microsoft Excel.

You get:
- Student-Planner.xlsx
- Start-Here-Guide.pdf

This is a digital download and nothing will be shipped. Grades are estimates from the weights you type, and your school's records always come first.

I designed this with the help of AI tools and checked the formulas with the example data before listing it.
```
