## Description

```
Pet care tracker for Google Sheets and Excel, for dogs, cats, rabbits and any pet you look after.

Dashboard:
- Vaccines and treatments overdue or due soon, the overdue ones first
- The food to buy, with the days left in every bag
- The vet visits booked
- What your pets cost this year, by category and by pet, with the vet bills

Pets:
- Each pet once: species, breed, birthday and age, chip, insurance and vet
- The latest weight and how it changed since the time before

Health:
- Vaccines, flea and worm treatments and check-ups, with how many months apart
- The next date, and a reminder as it comes closer

Vet visits:
- Every visit, past or booked: why, what the vet said and what it cost

Weight:
- Weigh each pet now and then, in any unit

Food:
- The bag size and how much a day: the day it runs out, when to buy the next one and what it costs a month

Spending:
- Everything you buy for them, by pet and by category

Room for 20 pets. Works in Google Sheets (free) and Microsoft Excel, in any currency. In Google Sheets you can share it with whoever helps look after them.

You get:
- Pet-Care-Tracker.xlsx
- Start-Here-Guide.pdf

This is a digital download and nothing will be shipped. The example pets, vets and shops are made up. How often a vaccine or treatment is needed depends on your pet and your country: ask your vet. It does not give veterinary advice.

I designed this with the help of AI tools and checked the formulas with the example data before listing it.
```
