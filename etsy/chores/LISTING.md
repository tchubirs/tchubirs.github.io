## Description

```
Chore chart and allowance tracker for Google Sheets and Excel: each child's chores with points, a chart to print for the fridge, and pocket money shared out into Save, Spend and Give jars.

Chores:
- Each chore for each child, with its points and the days it is expected
- A box for each day on This week, with the points and the pay added up
- A chart for each child to print, with the days to do each chore shaded

Pocket money:
- A base amount a week and so much a point, set for each child
- The week's pay worked out from the points
- Each amount split into Save, Spend and Give by the child's own shares, or put in one jar
- A goal for each child, with how much is saved and the weeks to go at the rate they save

Dashboard:
- The chores done this week, the points and what to pay each child
- What is in each jar, with a chart
- Each child's goal

Room for 6 children, 40 chores and 500 money lines. Works in Google Sheets (free) and Microsoft Excel, in any currency.

You get:
- Chores-Allowance-Tracker.xlsx
- Start-Here-Guide.pdf

This is a digital download and nothing will be shipped. The example family and their money are made up.

I designed this with the help of AI tools and checked the formulas with the example data before listing it.
```
