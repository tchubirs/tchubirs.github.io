## Description

```
Workout log and progress tracker for Google Sheets and Excel, for strength training at the gym or at home.

Log:
- One line per exercise, with the weight and reps of up to five sets
- Bodyweight exercises too: leave the weight empty and type the reps
- The best set, an estimated max and the volume of each exercise fill in by themselves
- A set that beats every earlier set of that exercise is marked New best

Plan:
- Which workout on which day, for example push, pull, legs, or upper and lower
- Up to six workouts with their exercises, sets and reps

Exercises:
- Your exercises with their muscle group
- How often you did each one, when you last did it, your best set and your estimated max

Dashboard:
- Workouts, sets and volume of the last 7 days
- The last 12 weeks, with a chart of the volume
- Sets per muscle group this week and the week before
- Your latest new bests
- Your weight now, since the start and to your goal, with a chart

Calendar:
- Pick a month and see every day you trained, with the name of the workout

Body:
- A weekly weigh-in, and waist, chest, hips, arm and thigh every few weeks

Works in lb or kg. Room for 1,500 log lines, 100 exercises and 400 weigh-ins. Works in Google Sheets (free) and Microsoft Excel.

You get:
- Workout-Log-Tracker.xlsx
- Start-Here-Guide.pdf

This is a digital download and nothing will be shipped. It is a training log and does not give medical or training advice.

I designed this with the help of AI tools and checked the formulas with the example data before listing it.
```
