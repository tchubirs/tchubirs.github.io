## Description

```
Timesheet and invoice maker for Google Sheets and Excel, for freelancers and anyone who bills by the hour.

Timesheet:
- One line per piece of work, with start and end times and a break, or the hours typed in
- The rate comes from the client or the project, or from the line itself for a fixed fee or a rush job
- Work that is not billable stays off every invoice
- Each line shows its invoice and whether it is still to invoice, invoiced or paid

Invoices:
- One line per invoice: its number, the client, the date and the dates of the work
- The work of that client in those dates goes on it by itself, with tax if you charge it
- The due date from each client's days to pay, and a warning when an invoice is overdue

Invoice:
- A page that fills itself when you pick the number, ready to print or save as a PDF
- One line per project and rate, the total hours, tax, total and how to pay

Hours report:
- Every line of the same invoice with its times, for a client who wants the detail

Dashboard:
- Hours this week and this month, and what the work is worth
- What is still to invoice, waiting or overdue, client by client
- The last six months: hours, worth and money paid in, with a chart

Projects:
- A rate of its own and a budget of hours for any project, with the hours left

Room for 2,000 lines of work, 300 invoices, 40 clients and 60 projects. Works in Google Sheets (free) and Microsoft Excel, in any currency.

You get:
- Timesheet-Invoice-Maker.xlsx
- Start-Here-Guide.pdf

This is a digital download and nothing will be shipped. The example studio, its clients and their hours are made up. This file does not give tax advice: check what an invoice needs to show where you work.

I designed this with the help of AI tools and checked the formulas with the example data before listing it.
```
