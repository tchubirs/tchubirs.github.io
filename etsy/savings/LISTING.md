## Description

```
Savings tracker for Google Sheets and Excel, for saving for several things at once: an emergency fund, a trip, gifts, a yearly bill, a new laptop.

Goals tab:
- One line per goal, with its target, its date if it has one, what you had saved before, and your plan per month
- What is saved, what is left and the progress of each goal
- What each goal still needs per month and per payday to be ready by its date (paid weekly, every 2 weeks, twice a month or monthly)
- A status for each goal: on track, short by a given amount a month, reached, or no date

Savings log:
- One line each time you put money in or take it out, with its goal

Month by month:
- Every goal and every month of the year in one grid
- Green when you put in your plan, orange for part of it, grey for nothing, red when more came out

Dashboard:
- Saved so far, target, left to save, progress and money put in this year
- What all your goals need a month and per payday
- A bar for each goal, a table and a chart by month
- Goals behind their plan, goals reached and the next target date

Two savings challenges:
- 52 week challenge: type x for each week you save, from low to high or high to low, with any amount for week 1
- 100 envelope challenge: type the number of each envelope you fill and the board turns green. It works with fewer envelopes too.

Room for 15 goals and 1,000 log lines. Works in Google Sheets (free) and Microsoft Excel, in any currency.

You get:
- Savings-Goals-Tracker.xlsx
- Start-Here-Guide.pdf

This is a digital download and nothing will be shipped. The file tracks numbers and does not give financial advice.

I designed this with the help of AI tools and checked the formulas with the example data before listing it.
```
