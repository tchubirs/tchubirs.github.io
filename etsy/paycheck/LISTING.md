## Description

```
Paycheck budget spreadsheet for Google Sheets and Excel. For people paid weekly, every two weeks, twice a month or monthly who want to know which bills each paycheck has to cover.

How it works:
- Type how often you are paid, your first payday and your usual paycheck
- List each bill once, with the day of the month it is due
- Each bill goes on the last paycheck before its due date, so that paycheck keeps the money for it

Paychecks tab:
- Your paydays for 12 months, listed for you
- For each paycheck: the bills it covers, savings, what is left to spend and how much per day until the next payday
- A status on each: OK, tight when less than your daily minimum is left, or short when the bills are more than the paycheck
- Type a different amount for a paycheck with overtime or a bonus

Dashboard:
- Opens on the paycheck you are on today, with its bills and due dates
- What is left to spend and how much per day
- Totals for the 12 months, and how many paychecks ahead are tight or short
- A chart of what is left from each paycheck

Room for 50 bills. Works in Google Sheets (free) and Microsoft Excel, in any currency.

You get:
- Paycheck-Budget-Planner.xlsx
- Start-Here-Guide.pdf

This is a digital download and nothing will be shipped. It is a budgeting tool and does not give financial advice.

I designed this with the help of AI tools and checked the formulas with the example data before listing it.
```
