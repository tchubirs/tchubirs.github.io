## Description

```
Baby log for Google Sheets and Excel, for the first year, from the first night home.

Dashboard:
- Today, or any day you pick: feeds, the last one and how long ago, sleep, wet and dirty nappies, and the next appointment
- The seven days before, with the average
- The latest weight, length and head, with the age
- A weight chart

Feeds:
- Breast (left, right or both) with the minutes, bottle with the amount, or solids

Sleep:
- When the baby fell asleep and woke up. A sleep past midnight counts on the day it started.

Nappies (diapers):
- Wet, dirty or both, with the time

Growth:
- Weight, length and head, with the age in weeks

Appointments:
- Checks and vaccines as they are booked

Milestones:
- First smile, rolling over, first tooth, first steps and more: type the date and the age fills in

Room for a whole year of feeds, sleeps and changes. Works in Google Sheets (free) and Microsoft Excel, in ml or oz, kg or lb, cm or inches. In the Google Sheets app you can add a feed from your phone at night, and share it with your partner.

You get:
- Baby-Log.xlsx
- Start-Here-Guide.pdf

This is a digital download and nothing will be shipped. The example baby and her days are made up. It is a log and it does not give medical advice.

I designed this with the help of AI tools and checked the formulas with the example data before listing it.
```
