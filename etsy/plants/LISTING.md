## Description

```
Plant care tracker for Google Sheets and Excel, for houseplants, balcony plants and herbs on the windowsill.

Today:
- The plants to water now, the late ones first, and the rest of the week
- The feeds due this week
- The repots coming up in the next two months

This week:
- Seven days ahead in a grid: water, feed or both, for every plant

Plants:
- Each plant once: its spot, its light, the days between waterings in the growing season and in the resting months, how often to feed it and when to repot it
- The last watering, the next one, and how many days late or ahead each plant is

Care log:
- One line each time you water, feed, mist, repot or prune
- The newest line of each kind sets the next date

Settings:
- The months your plants grow. Outside them, watering slows down and feeding stops. It works south of the equator too.

Room for 60 plants and 1,500 care lines. Works in Google Sheets (free) and Microsoft Excel. In the Google Sheets app you can log a watering from your phone.

You get:
- Plant-Care-Tracker.xlsx
- Start-Here-Guide.pdf

This is a digital download and nothing will be shipped. The example plants and their nicknames are made up. The days between waterings are a starting point: check the soil, and change them to suit your home.

I designed this with the help of AI tools and checked the formulas with the example data before listing it.
```
