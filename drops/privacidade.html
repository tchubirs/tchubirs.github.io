Privacy policy: DropGo

Last updated: November 4, 2026.

What the extension uses

  • Your Twitch session in this browser (the twitch.tv login cookie), only to ask Twitch for your drop progress, find channels with the campaign and claim drops at 100%.
  • Only while it reads drop campaigns, some headers of the requests the Twitch page itself makes to gql.twitch.tv: the "Client-Integrity" check Twitch requires to read drop campaigns, and session, device and version identifiers. The login is not copied from these headers. They stay only in the open page's memory, are never written to disk, are cleared afterwards and are only reused in requests to Twitch.
  • If you enable Kick (optional, with your permission): your Kick session in this browser, only to ask Kick for your drop progress and pick a stream of the game. You claim Kick rewards yourself in your inventory.
  • Only Twitch and Kick tabs. The extension has no access to the address or content of your other tabs, or to your browsing history.
  • Your choices and the extension state (on or off, chosen games, displayed progress, a history of its last 100 actions and, if you want, your Discord webhook address), stored only in your browser (chrome.storage).

Where requests go

  • Twitch (twitch.tv, gql.twitch.tv, id.twitch.tv): drop queries and claims for your own account.
  • Kick (kick.com, web.kick.com), only if you enable it: drop queries for your own account and the game's live streams. Reward images come from Kick itself.
  • GitHub: every 6 hours the extension downloads a public configuration file (config.json) with the technical codes of the Twitch queries and, when there is one, the text, image and link of the sponsored spot. This request carries no data of yours. The file contains data only, not code.
  • Discord: only if you paste a webhook from your Discord; then the extension sends your channel the claimed-item, completion or session-ended messages.
  • Item thumbnails are public images from Twitch itself (static-cdn.jtvnw.net).

What the extension does not do

  • It never sees or stores your password. It never sends the session token or headers to anyone other than Twitch (and, if enabled, Kick).
  • It never downloads or runs external code: all logic ships inside the extension.
  • It has no server of its own and sends no data of yours to the developer.
  • No tracking, no usage analytics, no selling or sharing of data.
  • The sponsored spot is just text with an image and a link shown in the extension window; the extension sends no data of yours to the sponsor.

Deleting

Removing the extension deletes everything it stored.

O que a extensão usa

Para onde vão pedidos

  • Twitch (twitch.tv, gql.twitch.tv, id.twitch.tv): as consultas e os resgates dos drops da sua própria conta.
  • Kick (kick.com, web.kick.com), só se você ativar: as consultas dos drops da sua própria conta e das lives do jogo. As imagens dos prêmios vêm da própria Kick.

  • Não vê nem guarda a sua senha. Não envia o token de sessão nem os cabeçalhos para ninguém além da Twitch (e, se ativada, da Kick).
  • Não baixa nem executa código de fora: toda a lógica vem dentro da extensão.
  • Não tem servidor próprio e não envia dados seus ao desenvolvedor.
